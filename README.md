🚀 About Me
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I'm a Member of Technical Staff at Foundry Robotics in San Francisco, CA, building AI-driven robot manipulation to help reshore American manufacturing. Previously, I was a Research Engineer at General Robotics Company and a researcher in Prof. Ken Goldberg's AUTOLAB at Berkeley AI Research (B.A. CS '24, M.S. EECS '25, UC Berkeley).

🌐 [tianshuangqiu.github.io](https://tianshuangqiu.github.io/) · 🎓 [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=an0V-t4AAAAJ)


💻 I'm Currently Working On

    🦾 Robot Learning for Manufacturing

    🤖 Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Policy Deployment

    🌊 Real-Time Action Chunking & Smooth Motion

    🤝 Compliant Control & Teleoperation

    👓 3D Vision & Scene Understanding
